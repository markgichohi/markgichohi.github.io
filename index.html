Water Services Database Analysis

SQL · MySQL · 8 related tables · 60,000+ records

Completed

Maji Ndogo survey data on 39,650 water sources serving 27.6 million people, with 60,146 field visits across 25 towns between January 2021 and July 2023. I used SQL to find where people lack safe, reachable water and where the data itself needs checking.

Data model

TableWhat it holds
locationAddress, province, town and type of each surveyed location
visitsEach field visit, with time in queue and the employee who made it
water_sourceType of each water source and the number of people it serves
water_qualitySubjective quality score recorded for each visit
well_pollutionChemical and biological contamination results for wells
employeeField staff who collected the survey data
global_water_accessCountry-level water service shares by year
data_dictionaryDescription and data type of every column

Key findings

  • Shared taps serve about 11.9 million people (43% of the total), and about 3.8 million people depend on broken indoor taps.
  • 12,403 of 17,383 tested wells (71.35%) are contaminated, affecting about 3.5 million people.
  • Shared taps average a 136.9-minute queue per visit against 17.0 minutes at rivers. Zuri has the longest town average at 118.5 minutes.
  • 64 wells marked "Clean" show bacterial contamination in their readings, which points to a data quality problem.

VLAN Routing Project

Cisco Packet Tracer · VLANs · Router-on-a-Stick · 802.1Q

Completed

A small office network split into HR, IT and Finance VLANs (192.168.10.0/24, .20.0/24 and .30.0/24) using one Cisco 2911 router, two 2960 switches and six PCs. Traffic between departments passes only through the router.

I tested same-VLAN and inter-VLAN connectivity, confirmed that shutting down the router link blocks inter-VLAN traffic, and used Simulation Mode to show how frames are tagged and how MAC addresses change at the router. A written report covers the configuration, test results and troubleshooting.
